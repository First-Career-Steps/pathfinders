Teaching the series with a team

Allow 60–75 minutes for the first two facilitated workbooks and 75–90 minutes for the last two. Demonstrate the worked example, let each participant try their own task, then compare the decisions they made. In Class 3, show exactly what travels between tools. Close each class by asking learners to share one thing they verified.
