Three situations a shift lead can practice

Lead the rush

Put one person in charge of the tickets and another in charge of guest updates. Practice the words both can repeat back.

Coach a new hire

Stop a wrong-bag handoff. Show the new hire the check, then watch them do the next pickup themselves.

Hand off the shift

A call-off leaves a gap at dinner. Give the incoming lead a name, a check time and a backup plan they can act on.

Use it at your store

Pick one course before the manager’s next shift. Have them try the case, read the feedback and write down what they’ll do on the floor. At the next changeover, ask what they said and what the crew did next.
