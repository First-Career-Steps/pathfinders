Separate supported work from open items

A close can keep moving while an exception stays open. Identify what evidence is missing, who can get it and which decision depends on it. Give reviewers the supported items first.

Worked exampleAn internal flash can label a number provisional, name the exception and give a time for the next update. Keep the review status tied to the evidence.
